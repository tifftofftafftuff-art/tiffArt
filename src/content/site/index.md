Hello! My name is Tiffany Sung, and I am an avid artist seeking a standard position within the production/entertainment field! I have a background in stage production, and currently designing my own artist identity. I am based in Georgia, United States. I am open to any upcoming projects, so please contact me for inquiries or even helpful criticism!

My art unifies my Korean heritage, silliness, and overall romance of this dark world. I have a dainty eye for bright colors, contrasting tones, and a heart for organic studies. I've been working through digital paintings, graphics, and production for five years. Recently, I have been snipping away with scissors and paper to develop paper collages and puppets. My form of expression is based in stories I day dream about, and I hope to find a team that feels the same!

My stage production experience, started off as being a performer open to endless possibilities. I have acted for a solid decade, through Columbus Highschool Theater, Springer Theatre Opera House, and Marist Theater. My love for ensemble and storytelling grew, and I wanted to explore other routes of production. I picked up roles in tech, publicity representative, directing, and even wrote a story of my own! Which was awarded best playwright at the 41st Annual Playwrights Festival at Marist University. I've directed for Marist Monologues: Wishing You Well, and Beauty and the Beast Bed and Breakfast. 

Outside of my pursuit, I find myself playing multi-player games with my friends and family, especially with Monopoly Deal and Codenames. I stay active by running, swimming, and will never turn down an opportunity to dance in public! I gain energy by being with others, and also appreciate the solitude of my own mindfulness. I can ramble about foreign movies, hip-hop, and caffeinated drinks. I recently picked up the guitar, and can play a couple of chords! 

I like to picture myself as a beginning gardener! I've planted my seeds, and have been observing the new sprouts to see how it can influence the other grounded plants my garden. I am working on an multi-media stop motion project for a couple weeks now. I am testing my skills in paper collage, puppet making, and digital set design throughout this process. In hopes, I can take advantages in more ways to tell stories!
